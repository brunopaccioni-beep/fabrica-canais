Privacy Policy

Fabrica Canais · last updated 16 October 2026

Who uses this app

Fabrica Canais is used only by its owner to manage YouTube channels that he owns. It is not offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Basic information about the authorized channel (name and ID), to make sure uploads go to the right channel.
  • The channel's own videos, playlists, captions and thumbnails, in order to upload and update them.
  • The channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, subscribers, audience retention), to write a private report for the owner.

The app does not access comments, messages, contacts, payment data, or any information about other people.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to report the owner's channel statistics back to him.
  • The authorization token and reports are stored only on the owner's personal computer. Nothing is stored on a server, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Analytics data kept in reports is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services policies.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google account security settings page (third-party connections). Revoking access stops the app immediately; the local token becomes useless.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support e-mail shown on the Google consent screen of this app.
